Logos desktop integrations for Logos Bible Software on Linux under [Ou Dedetai](https://github.com/FaithLife-Community/OuDedetai), for KDE Plasma 6 and GNOME.

## New in 0.5.2

- **Bible shortcuts for your top 15 Bibles.** Logos counts manuscripts and fragments as Bibles, so a large library can have hundreds. `o<abbr>` and `s<abbr>` (e.g. `oesv`, `othgnt`, `onkjv`) are now made for your top 15: Logos's Prioritize list, then the ones you open most, plus any listed in `picker_bibles`. Change the number with `bible_aliases` in `~/.config/logos-plasma/commands.toml`. Each shortcut is `o` plus the Bible's Logos abbreviation, so `okjv1900` is KJV 1900. `o esv John 3:16`, with a space, still works for any Bible.
- **The right Bible when abbreviations clash.** When two Bibles share an abbreviation (the ESV and an ESV fragment, say), the one you've prioritized or open most is used. Before, `oesv` could pick a resource you don't use, and Logos would open your preferred Bible instead.
- **`oesv` on its own** opens that Bible where you left off.
- **`osm`** opens the Sermon Manager.

## Install or update

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FaithLife-Community/logos-desktop-integrations/main/get.sh | sh

Then check everything with `python3 ~/.local/share/logos-plasma/logos_plasma.py --selftest`.
